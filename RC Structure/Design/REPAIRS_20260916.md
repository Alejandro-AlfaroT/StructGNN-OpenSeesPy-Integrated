# Verification and export repairs — 16 September 2026

Repairs applied to the reviewed `235c9ada` working tree. No engineering
assertions were enabled, `GENERATION_RELEASE_READY` remains False, and no
design sweep, calibration run, NTHA or SAP application was launched.

## Implemented

1. Restored the slab-completion context fields and early demand-declaration
   validation lost in the merge. Existing failure counterexamples pass again.
2. Corrected SAP Y-beam local-to-global moment mapping, preserved every saved
   gravity/seismic/live-pattern combination and live-pattern load case, and
   preserved the subdivision count per slab edge. Member weight modifiers
   remove slab/beam/column overlaps without reducing the section stiffness.
   Stiffness and importance factors come from saved evidence/inputs.
3. SAP comparisons no longer interpret steel areas or combination names as
   utilization ratios. Missing tables, missing/nonfinite ratios and incomplete
   ratio coverage are reported as unavailable/incomplete, not a numerical pass.
   Joint units rows containing radians are handled.
4. Verification reuse checks the actual artifact's complete request identity,
   schema, geometry, hazard, PROBE mode/date and (for new results) file SHA256.
   Qualification is recomputed from the record rather than trusting the old
   `accepted` label. Summaries reject duplicate/mismatched cases and cannot
   count a missing design as accepted. Worker attempt identifiers prevent a
   crash from returning a previous attempt's result.
5. Local launcher and worker OS leases exclude competing processes. Design
   locks and partial files are never automatically deleted. New PROBE roots
   require an explicit common date; plan contents are checked against their
   digest. Invalid counts, empty selections and negative offsets are rejected.
6. Compiled samples now bind the analysis status by SHA256; missing status or
   a changed status invalidates reuse. The scheduler checks this before
   counting successful or collapse-labelled runs as complete. Legacy collapse
   metadata also requires matching completed/requested steps; legacy samples
   without a digest retain timestamp-based freshness checks.
7. Corrected the dormant rectangular-column Y-axis probable-strength path:
   the concrete width/depth rotate with the steel layers. A rectangular column
   without its orthogonal layer layout now fails closed. The current square
   section ladder is unchanged.

## Limits and next validation

approval. SAP import and analysis remain untested in the application. Inspect
the imported point moments, mesh connectivity, weight modifiers and selected
design combinations before relying on its comparisons. P-Delta treatment and
spatial mass mapping still differ or require explicit verification; matching
total weight does not establish modal equivalence. DCR comparisons alone do
not close the member-force, floor-load-path or detailing assertions.

root when the revised design verification is authorized. Do not relabel them
